# The North Star

## Why Potential Exists

Every interview has the power to change someone's life.

A single conversation can open the door to a career, a new opportunity, or a fresh start.

Yet interviews often fail to reveal what people are genuinely capable of.

Not because they lack ability.

But because they are nervous.

Because they forget important examples.

Because they underestimate their own contribution.

Because the interviewer runs out of time.

Because the right follow-up question is never asked.

Potential exists to reduce those missed opportunities.

Not by helping candidates perform better than they are.

Not by making hiring decisions.

Not by lowering standards.

But by helping interviewers uncover stronger, more trustworthy evidence of genuine capability.

---

## Our North Star

Every candidate deserves a fair opportunity to demonstrate what they are genuinely capable of.

Every interviewer deserves the support to collect the evidence needed to understand that capability fairly.

Potential exists to make both of those statements more true.

---

## The Guiding Question

Every feature in Potential should answer one question:

> Have we given this candidate a fair opportunity to demonstrate what they are genuinely capable of?

If the answer is no...

Potential should help the interviewer create that opportunity.

---

## What Success Looks Like

A successful interview is not one where the AI speaks the most.

It is one where:

- the interviewer understands the candidate better
- the candidate feels heard
- important experiences were explored
- missing evidence was identified
- thoughtful follow-up questions were asked
- the final decision is based on richer evidence

---

## What Potential Will Never Become

Potential will never become an autonomous hiring system.

It will never:

- rank candidates
- score candidates
- recommend hiring decisions
- replace interviewers
- invent evidence
- help candidates fabricate experience

Human judgement always remains responsible for hiring decisions.

---

## What Potential Will Always Do

Potential will:

- help uncover genuine capability
- work only from observed evidence
- explain its reasoning
- encourage richer conversations
- identify evidence gaps
- help interviewers ask better questions
- respect the candidate
- respect the interviewer

---

## The Product Promise

Potential doesn't decide who deserves an opportunity.

Potential helps ensure every candidate has a fair opportunity to demonstrate why they deserve one.

---

## Every Future Feature Must Pass

Before any feature is built, ask:

Does this help reveal genuine capability?

Does it improve interview quality?

Does it preserve human judgement?

Does it strengthen trust?

If not, we don't build it.

Potential exists because too many capable people leave interviews feeling they never had the chance to show what they could really do.
